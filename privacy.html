Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information Prime Advo collects through this website, why we collect it and what you can do about it. Prime Advo is a marketing agency based in Madhya Pradesh, India.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business name and details you choose to share in your message
  • The service, package and budget range you select

Why we collect it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a proposal, schedule a discovery call and manage the working relationship if you become a client. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use this website, for example which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive. This data is aggregated and helps us improve the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to manage any engagement, and we take reasonable steps to keep them secure and limit access to our team.

Your rights and deletion requests

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. To request deletion, email info@primeadvo.site and we will act on your request within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy
